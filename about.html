Meet Cynthia Wambui Kimani

Founder & CEO

Her Accessori was founded by Cynthia Wambui Kimani, a woman with a lifelong taste for fashion and, above all, jewelry. What began as a personal passion for delicate, well-chosen pieces grew into a boutique dedicated to helping every woman wear her own kind of beautiful.

As CEO, Cynthia leads the boutique the way she lives: with an eye for detail, a love of elegance, and total commitment to her clients. Every piece that enters the collection is chosen personally, and nothing ships that she wouldn't wear herself.

She is, in every sense, a boss. But ask her what matters most and she'll tell you it's her clients. Your feedback shapes what she stocks, how she delivers, and how the boutique grows. At Her Accessori, the client is not just served. She is heard.
